Our story

A bakery built in public

Glaze is run by Ollie, who films the day-in-the-life of the bakery — early mornings, laminated pastry, and the counter filling up — on @ollieglaze_.

The shop account, @glazebakerysta, is where the bakes, collabs and street-party days are posted. Recent collabs have included pizza nights with Gracey’s Pizza, with bakes, slices and drinks on George Street.

If you have only seen Glaze on Instagram, this page is the missing piece: the address, the hours, and what to ask for when you get there.
